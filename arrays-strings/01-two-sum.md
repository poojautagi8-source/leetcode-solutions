# Problem: Two Sum (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/

## Approach

I used a nested-loop approach to check every possible pair of numbers. For each pair, I checked whether their sum was equal to the target value.

## Complexity

- Time: O(n²)
- Space: O(1)

## Notes

The solution returns the indices of the two numbers whose sum equals the target. An important edge case is when duplicate values are used at different indices.
